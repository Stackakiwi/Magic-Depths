Meet your next swing.

01

Choose any visible ring

Hold on a grapple ring to choose it—even behind Rook or at the far edge of the screen. Hold empty space for the easy automatic option.

02

Release to swing

Let go to carry your momentum. Rook can swing backward too; the chain stays attached while you hold.

03

Switch your anchor

Release and hold another ring, or drag your held finger onto it. Gold and gems are treasure; red crystal traps are hazards.

04

Find your rhythm

Release during the lower sweep for a clean swing. Three in a row earn ×2 treasure; six earn ×3. Each ring rewards you once per run.

05

Crystal Rush

Gems, coins, and clean swings charge a seven-second bonus: extra speed, double treasure, and protection. Traps fold away in the bonus stretch. No extra button.

06

Crystal Flight

Swing into the rare winged blue crystal. Fly head-first for 10 seconds: hold to rise and release to dive. A shield absorbs one hit during flight. When flight ends, hold to auto-grapple or choose a ring. The pickup activates immediately, even during Rush. Any remaining Rush time pauses and resumes after flight.

07

A little room to find your swing

The first 500 m have no obstacles. Rings and treasure are still there, so you can find your rhythm. After 500 m, watch for crimson crystal traps above and below. You still need to catch rings to avoid falling.

08

A little guardian, two deliveries

Bring the sapphire in Rook’s chest to the Sapphire Shrine at 10,000 m. He then carries a ruby to the Ruby Shrine at 20,000 m. The chest gem changes automatically; fly or swing through either destination. After both deliveries, the endless run continues. A new run starts with sapphire again.
